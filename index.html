HyMd 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

HyMd（以下称"本应用"）是一款在 Windows 上本地运行的 Markdown 编辑器，由 zhy 开发并通过 Microsoft Store 发布。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 我们不收集个人信息

本应用不要求注册或登录账号，不收集、不存储、不上传你的姓名、邮箱、设备标识、位置或任何其他个人信息，也不包含广告、统计分析或遥测（telemetry）组件。

2. 你的文档只保存在本机

你创建、打开和编辑的文件，以及应用设置，都只保存在你的电脑上，由你自己选择的位置决定。本应用不会把这些内容发送到我们或任何第三方的服务器。文字识别（OCR）等功能也在本机完成，不经过云端。

3. 由你主动发起的网络访问

如果你的文档引用了网络图片或其他在线资源，或者你点击了文档中的链接，本应用或你的浏览器会直接访问对应的网站，这和普通浏览网页一样，相关网站可能按其自身政策处理访问信息。本应用不会记录或转发这些访问。

4. 应用内购买

本应用的应用内购买由 Microsoft Store 处理。付款和账户信息由 Microsoft 依据 Microsoft 隐私声明 收集和处理，我们无法获取你的支付信息。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

6. 政策变更

如果本政策有更新，我们会修改本页面并更新生效日期。

7. 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，可以通过 Microsoft Store 中 HyMd 页面上的支持联系方式与我们联系。

HyMd Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

HyMd ("the app") is a Markdown editor that runs locally on Windows, developed by zhy and distributed through the Microsoft Store. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. We do not collect personal information

The app requires no account or sign-in. It does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, device identifiers, location, or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry components.

2. Your documents stay on your device

Files you create, open, and edit, along with app settings, are stored only on your computer in locations you choose. The app does not send this content to our servers or to any third party. Features such as text recognition (OCR) run entirely on your device.

3. Network access you initiate

If a document references online images or other web resources, or you click a link in a document, the app or your browser connects directly to that website, just like ordinary web browsing. Those sites may handle the request under their own policies. The app does not log or forward these requests.

4. In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Microsoft Store. Payment and account information is collected and handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; we never receive your payment details.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support contact listed on HyMd's Microsoft Store page.
